## 1. About the Service

Mumu Games is a cognitive training game for children aged 3 to 6. It offers six cognitive activities,
a 24-dinosaur encyclopedia and a six-chapter story.
The app is delivered as a Progressive Web App and can be installed to the home screen of a supported browser.

We may update features, expand the question bank or refine the interface from time to time.
Major changes will be announced on this page.

## 2. How to Use

- The app is designed for children aged 3 to 6 to use **with a parent or caregiver**.
- Sessions of 5 to 15 minutes work best. Long continuous play is not recommended.
- Children do not need, and should not, create an account or enter any personal information.
- Please use the app in a suitable environment with moderate volume and adequate lighting.

## 3. Responsibilities of Parents

- Please assist your child with the controls and gently discuss what each activity is showing.
- If your child becomes tired or frustrated, take a break. There is no need to finish every level.
- Encyclopedia unlocks and stars are game design elements only. They carry no evaluative meaning.

## 4. Data and Privacy

The full details are in our Privacy Notice. The key points are:

- Progress, stars and encyclopedia unlocks are stored **only on your device** and are never uploaded by us.

- The three consent settings (analytics, personalization, marketing) can be withdrawn separately at any time.
- You can use "Delete all data" at any time to erase the complete local save.
